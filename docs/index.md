## DNDBot Documentation

Welcome to the DNDBot docs. This bot is a Discord companion for running D&D games, with tight integration to DiceCloud and quality‑of‑life tools for players and DMs.

### What this bot does

- **Profile sync (DiceCloud)**: Pulls your character sheet from DiceCloud and keeps key stats (AC, HP, portrait, etc.) mirrored locally for quick access.
- **Ammo tracking**: Tracks ammo in three buckets per character – **quiver**, **stash**, and **fired** – and keeps them in sync with DiceCloud ammo when requested.
- **Attack integration**: Lets you use `!attack` in a way that feels similar to Avrae, auto‑decrementing ammo for ranged weapons.
- **Calendar & campaigns**: Provides a simple in‑world calendar helper for your campaign, with support for multiple campaigns.
- **Utility commands**: Help, who‑am‑I, and developer helpers.
